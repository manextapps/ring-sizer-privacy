Privacy Policy, Ring Sizer

Effective date: November 6, 2026

In short

  • Ring Sizer has no account, no sign in and no server of our own. Your measurements never reach us.
  • The card check never reads your card. The app only stores how many screen pixels make one millimetre, on your phone. It has no camera access.
  • Your saved rings (a name you type and a size), the screen calibration and your settings are stored only on your device.
  • Ads in Ring Sizer come from Google's ad SDK (Google AdMob). Google collects certain data, such as your IP address and advertising ID, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. This is described below.
  • Where the law requires consent (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), you are asked before personalized ads are used, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings, Privacy choices.
  • Every feature is free. An optional one time purchase removes all ads. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play.

1. Who we are

Ring Sizer: Measure Ring Size (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is published by manextapps. This policy explains how the Android app handles information. For questions, see Contact.

2. About the app

Ring Sizer finds a ring size from a ring you already own. You check your screen once against a standard bank or ID card, lay the ring on the screen and match a circle to its inside edge. The app shows the inner diameter and the size in the US, UK, EU and Japanese systems. You can also type a diameter or a circumference, save results under a name and look up the size chart.

All of this works offline. An internet connection is used only for ads, for the ad consent form, for the optional purchase, and when you choose to open a link such as this policy.

3. Data we collect

We, the developer, do not operate a server for this app and do not collect, receive or store personal data about you. There is no account or sign in, no analytics or crash reporting tool of our own, and no Firebase.

Google, which provides the ads, collects some data directly through its software development kit (SDK). That is explained in section 5. The optional purchase is processed by Google Play, see section 8.

If you email us, we will receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply and keep it only as long as needed for that purpose.

4. Data stored on your device

So the app works, it saves the following information locally on your device. It is not sent to us.

  • Your saved rings: the name you give each one (for example “Mom's ring”), its inner diameter and the date you saved it.
  • The screen calibration: a single number for how many pixels make one millimetre on your screen, plus the screen width it was made on.
  • The last circle size you used, and whether the light background is on.
  • Counters used to pace ads (how many measurements you finished and when the last full screen ad showed).
  • Settings: sound, vibration, reduced motion, whether you finished the first run guide, and whether ads were removed by a purchase.

Google's ad, consent and billing libraries also store some information on your device, such as your consent choices, so that they work correctly.

5. Advertising and Google's ad SDK

Ring Sizer uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to show ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request ad consent where required. The app shows:

  • A banner ad at the bottom of the size chart and the saved rings list. There is no banner while you calibrate, measure or read your result.
  • Full screen (interstitial) ads, only after a finished measurement when you tap “Measure another ring”. Never during your first measurement, never while you calibrate or measure, at most once each time you open the app, and at least three minutes after the app started or the last one.

There are no rewarded ads. Both kinds of ads stop after the remove ads purchase.

Non personalized ads are based on more limited context, such as the app itself and your general (not precise) location. Depending on your choices, your region and your device settings, Google may show you personalized, non personalized or limited ads. Learn more in How Google uses cookies and similar technologies in advertising.

6. Consent and your ad choices

Where it is required (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and, where enabled, certain U.S. states), the app shows Google's consent message when it starts. You can accept, decline or manage your choices.

When privacy options are available for your region, you can review or change your choices at any time in the app under Settings, Privacy choices. If your choices mean ads cannot be requested, the app still works the same way.

You can also manage ad personalization for your Google Account at My Ad Center.

7. Your advertising ID

Android provides a resettable advertising ID. On most devices you can reset or delete it in Settings, Privacy, Ads (menu names vary by device and Android version). Deleting it stops apps from accessing it for advertising; resetting it replaces it with a new random ID. Some devices also offer ad privacy settings for the Android Privacy Sandbox in the same area.

8. In app purchase

You can buy a one time “Remove ads” upgrade. The purchase is made through Google Play Billing: Google handles the payment and your payment details, and we never see them. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play that the upgrade belongs to your Google account, so it can turn off the banner and full screen ads and restore the purchase on a new device (Settings, Restore purchase). Google's handling of purchases is described in the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play terms.

9. The card check

To draw the measuring circle at its true size, the app asks you to hold a card of the standard bank card size (85.60 by 53.98 mm) on the screen and match an outline to it. The app never reads, photographs or stores anything about the card. It has no camera permission, and you are welcome to cover the card number with your thumb. The only result is the size of the outline you matched, saved on your phone as a pixels per millimetre value. This value is excluded from device backup because it only fits the screen it was made on.

10. Android permissions

Ring Sizer does not ask you for runtime permissions such as location, camera, contacts or storage. The installed app includes the following permissions, which are added by Google's libraries:

  • Internet access and network state: to load ads, the consent form and the purchase screen.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): lets the ad SDK read the advertising ID.
  • Android ad services (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS): let the ad SDK use Android's Privacy Sandbox advertising features where the device supports them.
  • Google Play billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): lets the app offer the optional purchase.
  • Wake lock and foreground service: required by the Android WorkManager library that the ad SDK uses for background tasks. Ring Sizer itself does not run a foreground service.
  • An internal receiver permission (com.manextapps.ringsizer.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION): added by the AndroidX library so that only this app can send messages to its own components. It gives no access to your data.

11. Children

Ring Sizer is a general audience tool for people buying or gifting rings. It is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app itself does not collect personal information from anyone. Names you give saved rings stay on your device. Ads in the app are served by Google, which collects the data described in section 5. If you have questions or concerns, please contact us.

12. Data retention and deletion

  • Our servers: we have none, so we keep no copy of your data.
  • On your device: your saved rings, calibration and settings stay until you delete a ring in the app, reset the calibration in Settings, clear the app's data in Android settings or uninstall the app.
  • Google: data collected by Google's ad SDK and by Google Play is retained according to Google's policies. See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google retains data.

13. Device backup

Ring Sizer lets Android's backup feature include your saved rings and settings, so they can be restored if you set up a new device. The screen calibration is never backed up, because a new phone needs its own card check. If backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may store this data in your Google Account backup or move it during a device to device transfer, under Google's terms and your device settings. Data stored by the ad and consent libraries is excluded from backup. You can turn backup off in your device's settings.

14. Sharing and links

We do not sell or share your personal data, because we do not receive any. Apart from Google's libraries described above, the app sends information only when you choose to:

  • Open a link, such as this privacy policy. This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This page uses no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts.
  • Contact us: the app opens your email app with our address filled in. You decide what to send.

15. Security

Because we do not collect your data, there is no copy of it on our side to protect or lose. Data on your device is protected by Android's app sandbox. Google states that the data its ad SDK collects is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

16. Your rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Israel's Protection of Privacy Law, 5741 (1981), and U.S. state privacy laws may give you rights over your personal data. These can include the right to access, correct or delete it, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority.

  • Since we hold no personal data about you, in most cases there is nothing for us to access or delete. Your data is on your device and you can delete it as described in section 12.
  • For data collected by Google, use your ad choices in the app (section 6), your device's ad settings (section 7), and Google's tools described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • You can always contact us, and we will help as far as we are able.

17. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the app or the services it uses change. We will post the updated version on this page and change the effective date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also take reasonable steps to let you know, for example through the app's release notes.
